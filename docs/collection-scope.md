# Source collection

The first edition reviews 140 X posts and three Hacker News context records. The initial research covers October 16 16:00 UTC through October 18 02:16:19 UTC. The follow-up covers October 18 02:16:19–12:00:05 UTC and returns 36 additional X posts. One directly linked primary post supplements the initial research.

X posts require more than 50 captured likes. Media is optional so code, writing and other textual tasks remain eligible. The incremental cap is 300 posts and was not reached. Only the declared queries and provider-visible records are covered.

The public collection selects 37 concrete cases in eight task categories. Same-project previews, reposts and follow-up measurements add source relationships. Release announcements, broad praise, free-access promotions and future plans do not become project cases. Two sources remain outside publication pending source or media review.

The six displayed Prompts are verbatim public source spans: four from root posts, one from an author reply and one from a creator's linked project collection. The latter is a selected brief from the 100-HTML experiment. Original Prompt language and punctuation are preserved.

All 40 selected source attachments have a public disposition. Image cases preserve every selected image; video cases use linked posters and playable MP4s; text-only cases preserve their task and original source. Engagement values retain their individual capture timestamps. Research archives stay private; publication does not include account-profile exports, signed URLs or collection caches.
